Multi-View Geometry: Classical vs Deep Feature Matching

Benchmarked 4 feature matching pipelines — SIFT, ORB, SuperPoint, and LightGlue — on the KITTI driving dataset across 5,600+ frames. Key finding: LightGlue's transformer-based matching matches SIFT-level pose accuracy (2.57 vs 2.55 degrees) on challenging low-texture highway scenes where classical binary descriptors degrade by over 270%.
